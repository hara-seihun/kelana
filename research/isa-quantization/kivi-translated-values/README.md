# Paid frozen translated KIVI2 V: negative held consumer result

The single train-frozen additive V center from [`../value-range-translation/`](../value-range-translation/README.md) **does not improve** the eight retained complete-O held outputs. Even though it lowers every group's train worst range, its pooled held squared error is **1.145785953**, versus unchanged K2/V2 **0.832657928**. A conventional unchanged-code V35 retention control is both **1,152 B smaller** and better (**0.775165228**); the translated arm is dominated on this panel. Already frozen K2/V4 gives **0.193585015** at a larger 361,856-B peak. This is a finite eight-state CPU result, not full-model generation or native latency evidence. No center/precision/recent-threshold selection followed scoring.

## Frozen arithmetic and paid layout

All four original held chronological arrival streams from [`../kivi-value-intern/`](../kivi-value-intern/) supply BF16 V and BF16 K; every `u16 t | 2048B K | 2048B V` arrival is admitted in order. The sole [`center-fp16.bin`](center-fp16.bin) is exactly **2,048 B**, SHA256 `143c78b481c35b7c14699825931e7ef68b8d7edbac4fa75775effdd095c7923c`, identical to the train-frozen source certificate. For each arriving original BF16 V coordinate: decode BF16 and FP16 center into FP32, subtract in FP32, **round once to BF16**, insert that centered BF16 record. The V quantizer is the original KIVI2 per-token four contiguous G32 groups: min and `(max−min)/3`, nearest-even unsigned 2-bit digit, FP16 low/step (48B/128D token). The query reads its preflush cache, then the original V recent33 threshold flushes its oldest; the original K2 codes, fields, BF16 recent K and K32 flush chronology remain byte-identical to their original event owner. The original Q and all 1,024×2,048 BF16 O coefficients are untouched.

The sole prepared [`bias-fp32.bin`](bias-fp32.bin) is **4,096 B**: decode original BF16 O and the FP16 center to FP32, repeat each KV-head center for its two Q heads, multiply once `b=O·repeat2(c)` in FP32. A reader uses centered V directly and adds this one 1,024-D bias to final FP32 O output (1,024 additions per query); no per-position output corrections are hidden. Normalized attention justifies the exact real-coordinate transport, but the BF16 centered insertion and FP32 arithmetic are separately measured rather than called exact. Per arriving token this pays **1,024 FP32 subtractions and 1,024 BF16 rounds**. Preparing b pays **2,097,152 FP32 multiply-accumulate terms**, an O read of **4,194,304 B** shared original BF16 weights, and in the literal CPU producer an **8,388,608-B FP32 expanded O** plus FP32 center/repeated-center/output scratch (at least 4,096/8,192/4,096 B respectively); those are preparation/transient memory, not free or a simultaneously charged persistent extra O matrix. The literal audit reader transiently expands two 8×256×128 FP32 K/V arrays (**2,097,152 B** at t256), plus Q/scores/mix. A native packed reader, timing or integrated live RAM has not been established.

One additional comparator uses original K2 and **byte-identical original V2 events**, delayed exactly three query times: V recent35 retained after each query, flush on the arrival taking it to36; no quantizer or field changes. It has no fitted center or bias. At t256 preflush its 8 heads retain 220 quantized V tokens and36 BF16 recent tokens/head versus translated 223/33. The immutable fixed fields and actual serialized cache bill:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cache peak before query | 304,768 B | 309,760 B | 304,768 B | 361,856 B |
| Center + output bias | 6,144 B | 0 B | 0 B | 0 B |

The static original O and other shared model weights are excluded equally. Audit event logs, source streams, manifests, Q/teacher endpoints and final images are research evidence, not concurrently resident inference-cache state. V35's +4,992-B per-sequence cache buys a better result than the translated candidate's +6,144-B model-fixed fields at this sequence count; the center/bias can be shared over sequences, so do not extend this one-sequence dominance claim to all concurrency levels.

## Eight held endpoints and independent checks

The only scored queries are four held streams × `t128,t256`, with the **same retained 16Q/8KV Q and teacher** and unchanged BF16 O from [`../kivi-two-bit-dot-native/snapshots.json`](../kivi-two-bit-dot-native/snapshots.json). Each Q, teacher, original O and original K2 image hash is checked; no checkpoint, source arrays, forward, GPU or missing Q are used. [`build.py`](build.py) emits actual eight-head final cache images and timestamped K/V logs for both fixed arms, plus all 4×256×8 before/after prefix SHA256/byte/count ledgers in `held-*-manifest.json`. [`check.py`](check.py) imports none of its quantizer/packing/decoding functions. It independently reads original events and BF16 arrivals, checks every original K2 K byte and independently recomputes K2 K/V digits and FP16 fields against the source available at each flush, reconstructs the single BF16 rounding and paid bias, checks every candidate event and every pre/post prefix and final image, and decodes the actual retained preflush images to compute full 16Q attention/complete O. It cross-checks the original K2/V2 output against the hashed retained conventional CPU output and pinned [`../kivi-kv-rate-exchange/results.json`](../kivi-kv-rate-exchange/results.json) receipt. K2/V4 is reused from that same pinned control, not refit.

| Original K2/V2 | 0.832657928 | 0.003068937 |

The **source-only BF16 boundary error** is isolated with the same K2 key probabilities and *unquantized original BF16 V* versus once-rounded centered BF16 V plus the one output bias: pooled complete-O squared difference **0.000751031** (squared norm / teacher² **0.00000276808**). Across all four held streams, the coordinate-level once-rounding squared sum is about **0.112255**, stored per-window in manifests. This direct unquantized source-only response is small. It does **not** isolate the effect of BF16 insertion rounding on the later nonlinear min/max extrema, digit assignments and stored FP16 fields. The paid translated program loses to V35 without a causal attribution of its degradation to any one stage. The complete per-state numerators and source-boundary scores are in [`results.json`](results.json).
