The programme's [polynomial packed-label continuation](https://lemma.ing/dev/programmes/12) (`canon/radix-polynomial/`) proves that a complete radix-`B` × `H` reachable rectangle needs degree at least `H(B−1)+1` to expose the low digit from its sole packed label by exact branchless arithmetic. The radix-7/seven-level tile therefore needs degree at least 43 and six multiplication sites/depth; a correlated seven-state producer instead permits a single affine scale. Lean and an integer interpolation witness live in programme canon. This bounds only the polynomial continuation grammar, not integer quotient, comparisons, native work or model behavior.

The [division-free Mersenne continuation](https://lemma.ing/dev/programmes/12) (`canon/mersenne-continuation/`, main `3043ed6129ecc57590323091d227b16ac99b2b61`) crosses that grammar boundary: for `q=a+(2^k−1)b`, `a<2^k−1`, `b≤2^k`, the exact high digit is `(q+(q>>k)+1)>>k`, and the low digit follows by shift/subtract/add. Lean proves the generic rectangle and sharp first failure; 87,418 finite checks cover radix instances. At radix 7 it extracts both digits on a 7×9 rectangle with no online multiply/divide/table after the packed dot. The one-use direct dot is the stronger control, and no native or model-quality speedup is claimed; the programme owns the detailed proof, code and successor question rather than a duplicate copy here.

Programme 12's [three-key winner ordinal](https://lemma.ing/dev/programmes/12) (`canon/multikey-argmax/`, main `54f095e`) extends the separately proved `canon/argmax-family/` signed-vote insertion quotient beyond its three-byte direct-lane horizon at 255 records. A gapless rank of signed-octahedron slices updates exactly through 292 records: 16,769,269 states fit 24 bits; at 293 records 16,941,554 force a fourth byte. Lean checks the threshold and C replays 18,408,390 complete low-length transitions plus 100,000 high-boundary updates. The new coordinate pays dependent slice search, division and repacking; real producer occurrence, native execution and model quality are open. Programme canon alone owns the proof and replay.

## Search the nearby executable maps

Write a candidate as `O(P(z,E(x)))`, where z is the stored model-specific description, E prepares the dynamic input, P is an ISA computation and O supplies the final observation or encoded continuation. No weight reconstruction is required unless a live consumer needs it.

The central search variables are coupled. A quantization change may remove a carry, permit a different accumulator, create a shared response, or make a continuation cheap in the producer's labels. A smaller code alphabet may instead destroy the distinction needed by a later consumer. Search complete regions before requiring source operations to survive individually.

Keep separate frontiers for behavioral distortion, complete static bytes and realized execution. Model-specific instruction constants and tables count as storage. Registers, dynamic packing, communication, synchronization and conversions count as work. An arbitrary free decoder can make almost any injective carrier look sufficient without making it useful.
