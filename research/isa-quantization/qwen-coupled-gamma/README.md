**Result, source only.** A single train-derived power-of-two reciprocal Q/K gamma gauge lowers the fixed source's uniform-causal-pair **norm-product** objective `F=E[(||q_rot||²/128)||k_rot||²]` from **285,598.5831 to 9,548.6321 train**, and from **285,707.4940 to 9,453.5279 inspected held** under the **same train-selected exponents**. ## Objective, preparation and one rounding decision

For each original post-Qnorm/RoPE query `q_{h,t}` and original post-Knorm/RoPE key `k_{g(h),i}`, let `Q_a=||q_{a,a+64}||²/128` and `K_b=||k_{b,b+64}||²` for each of the 64 RoPE pairs. Under equal weight across the **8 train windows, all16 Q heads, every causal pair `0≤i≤t<256`**, [`prepare.py`](prepare.py) accumulates the full positive 64×64 matrix

`C_ab = (1/(8×16×32896)) Σ_window,h,t Σ_{i≤t} Q_a(h,t) K_b(g(h),i)`.

It uses causal **prefix sums of key pair energy** rather than materializing ~4.21 million 64×64 pair outer products. [`train-pair-moment.npy`](train-pair-moment.npy) stores this FP64 *offline* statistic (matrix payload 32,768 B); `prepare.json` pins its SHA256. The corresponding [`held-pair-moment.npy`](held-pair-moment.npy) is computed **only after the train gauge was frozen** for diagnosis, never optimizer input. This measure is uniform over pairs, **not** equal query then equal prior, token-ID law, softmax-weighted importance, or model generation frequency.

For real exponents `x_a`, the positive convex diagonal-balance objective modulo a common shift is `F(x)=Σ_ab C_ab·4^(x_a−x_b)`. Its exact analytic gradient is `ln4 [4^x_a Σ_b C_ab4^(−x_b) − 4^(−x_a)Σ_b C_ba4^x_b]`. One SciPy BFGS run fixed `x_0=0`, took **127 iterations**, returning `F=9,114.11668193`, absolute reduced-gradient maximum **1.5036e−5** (relative to F **1.65e−9**). SciPy flagged precision loss rather than success at the stricter requested `gtol=1e−7`; this is a **near-stationarity numerical receipt, not an exact global optimum certificate**, and no restarted solver or damping sweep was run.

The *single joint* integer rounding examines the at-most64 distinct open intervals induced by thresholds `u=1−frac(x_a)` in `[0,1)`; each candidate `e_a=floor(x_a+u)` is normalized by subtracting `e_0`. [`prepare.json`](prepare.json) records all **64 unique integer candidates**, their objective, and the train-only minimum at `u=.32683809657`; lower-u ties are deterministic. The selected `e` counts are `−1:1, 0:7, 1:51, 2:3, 3:1, 4:1`. Its **train F=9,548.63212843** is 1.047675 times the numerical continuous value, not an assertion of the parent's ideal-theory bound relative to an exactly certified optimizer. [`geometry.py`](geometry.py) measures held baseline `285,707.49397`, chosen F `9,453.52790` (ratio .0330881); train chosen/baseline ratio .0334338. No held-selected exponent/interval or feature/codebook/seed/bit adjustment.

The motivation is specific: after averaging over a Haar coarse rotation and independent Gaussian QJL residual, the parent calculation gives a conditional score-variance norm-product term `c₁||q||²||k||²` plus a dot-square term; reciprocal Q/K scaling keeps the original dot product and its square invariant. The complete coupled `C_ab` retains query/key energy correlations across different RoPE pairs. Merely balancing marginal Q/K variances separately would drop those correlations. This is a **risk-geometry diagnostic**, not the true complete-O loss or an exact finite-storage variance guarantee; the original Qwen source's role is explicit. A subsequent [exact integer certificate](../integer-gauge-balance/README.md) proves that this already chosen e globally minimizes the stored dyadic C objective over all integer exponents. It changes no gain and does not turn the FP64 source statistic into an unrounded moment or an output-risk optimum.

## Paid image, source equivalence and boundaries

Qwen Q/K RMSNorm gamma is applied *after* per-head normalization. For each pair `(a,a+64)`, the two Qgamma fields are multiplied by `2^e_a` and two Kgamma fields by `2^(−e_a)`. Pair-equal gains commute with Qwen's position RoPE; all sixteen Q heads share Qgamma and all eight KV heads share Kgamma. [`replacement-qk-gamma.bf16`](replacement-qk-gamma.bf16), SHA256 `dd1b5b537c145a4113bdc41639de86d3875e3434125a34483d89ead581089d40`, is the **actual 512-byte little-endian BF16 replacement image** (Q128 fields first, K128 fields second), not a free abstract exponent table. Its original concatenated gamma image has SHA256 in [`prepare.json`](prepare.json). [`reader.py`](reader.py) is independent of the optimizer: it reads bytes, checks both original checkpoint BF16 gamma arrays and ratios, reconstructs exactly the same reciprocal integer powers with no zero/subnormal/overflow/nonfinite field, and verifies each replacement field equals the original multiplied by its stated power **without BF16 rounding error**. The offline C matrices, numerical optimizer and exponent list are not runtime metadata. Static full-layer Q/K/V/O/gamma source state **12,583,424 B** remains the same allocation with gamma *replaced*, not added; K/V cache and position counters are unchanged.

[`observe.py`](observe.py) independently reprojects original full Q16/K8/V8 from the pinned fixture/checkpoint, reads the new gamma image through `reader.py`, reruns canonical BF16 Q/K RMSNorm and position RoPE, all16×256×256 causal logits/log-softmax/probabilities, the original values and full O. It verifies both original projections reproduce the immutable [full source](../skvq-global-gqa/source.py) and compares modified rotary Q/K against their exact scaled original counterparts before scoring. [`aggregate.py`](aggregate.py) requires **every** window to have `q,k,score,prob,full O` all bitwise-equal and combines original full-O denominators; all reported differences are exactly zero. In the first diagnostic implementation, `softmax(logits)` was compared to the original canonical `exp(log_softmax(logits))`, which caused ordinary floating reduction discrepancies even with identical scores. The retained acceptance uses the **same canonical `log_softmax().exp()` path** on both; no gauge/parameter was revised. Exact CPU BF16 replay is not a native GPU instruction-level bitwise claim nor a modified whole-model checkpoint deployment.
