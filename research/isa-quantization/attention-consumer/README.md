# Qwen q_proj: the live causal attention observer

**Comparable-size scope:** the 8,128/8,384-byte signed images are smaller than the 8,704-byte local Q4 control. Their measured accuracy losses and larger nominal work do not constitute a universal comparable-size family rejection or SOTA comparison. The exact Q/K sign symmetry and complete-consumer diagnostics remain valid; see [the evaluation contract](../BRIEF.md#comparable-size-state-of-the-art-is-the-target).

**Result.** The *observer* is coarser than Euclidean Q: a paired Q/K sign relabeling changes held raw-Q relative squared error from **0.00174 to 1.02018** while leaving every measured score, attention KL and post-O response unchanged (maximum score difference zero). But this symmetry does **not** rescue the 8,128-byte signed-carrier96 image rejected at the raw-Q subprojection. On actual Qwen causal histories, it has held head-0 attention KL **0.07124** and post-O relative squared error **0.09637**, versus the 8,704-byte exported affine Q4 image's **0.001805** and **0.002262**. A fitted, serialized 256-byte *joint K-coordinate correction* reduces signed head-0 KL to **0.06670** but still loses badly and damages the second query head sharing that key head. Even allowing this K family **unpaid arbitrary real coefficients**, its training teacher-probability-weighted centered-score least-squares floor is **30.99×** the direct Q4 score loss. The 8,384-byte corrected image is smaller than Q4 by 320 static bytes but does more online work. This is a measured failure to match the larger Q4 accuracy for two actual candidates, **not** a universal lower bound over joint Q/K encodings.

The prior [attention-metric study](../../quantization-discovery/subbit/attention-metric/README.md) already established a full-Q-model factor whose raw Q response worsened while its attention behavior improved. This study does not claim rediscovery: it transports the [ISA producer-screen's actual 128×128 paid images](../producer-screen/README.md) across their *previously missing* attention boundary, proves a simultaneous Q/K gauge and tests a newly paid K-side continuation including the collateral GQA consumer.

## Exact observer distinctions

For query position `t`, visible keys `j≤t`, normalized and RoPE-rotated vectors `q_t,k_j`, logits are `ℓ_tj=q_t·k_j/√128`. Softmax sees only differences `ℓ_tj−ℓ_t0`: a constant per query over its visible keys cancels exactly. A positive common Q-head rescaling is also an exact RMSNorm gauge only at epsilon zero, and is approximate at Qwen's `1e−6` epsilon; arbitrary row gains are not gauges. The value/O endpoint sees `Σ_j softmax(ℓ_t)_j v_j W_O`, which can identify still fewer distributions if their difference annihilates the live value/O map. Report KL and post-O error **separately**. Neither raw Q norm nor equal fibers establishes either.

Qwen's RoPE rotates coordinate pairs `(i,i+64)`. Simultaneously changing the signs of **both** coordinates of any pair in normalized Q and K commutes with every RoPE position and preserves each dot product. Sign reversal also commutes with per-head RMSNorm (including positive epsilon), its diagonal gamma and BF16 rounding. For Qwen's GQA, key head 0 feeds **Q heads 0 and 1**, so the gauge must change both query heads as well as the key head. The transformation can be baked into already-stored signs: each affine Q4 row's FP16 origin and step changes sign, the remaining BF16 Q rows and the BF16 K rows change sign. No extra model field or online multiplication is needed. The experiment applies the transformed numeric vectors to head 0; its zero score difference is an exact observed arithmetic check. It does not pretend changing only head-0 Q and shared K would leave head 1 unchanged. [`Kelana/AttentionConsumer.lean`](../../../Kelana/AttentionConsumer.lean) proves pairwise and whole-head sign/rotation score invariance, row-offset centering invariance and the scalar stationary-quadratic identity without `sorry`. The 128-dimensional numerical Gram calculation is not itself exported as a kernel-checked certificate. The real softmax cancellation follows by factoring `exp(offset)` from numerator and denominator; that analytic exponential identity, BF16 kernel lowering, and native instruction costs are outside this Lean module.

A general invertible Q/K change `q→Aq`, `k→A^{-T}k` preserves an unrotated bilinear score. To preserve a *shared static pre-RoPE* coordinate system at every position, the changes must intertwine each RoPE matrix, and to descend through RMSNorm/gamma and quantized hardware they need additional laws. An arbitrary orthogonal matrix is not therefore a free ISA symmetry. The paired sign is a concrete legal member, not an arbitrary relabeling.

## Complete measured path and price

[`measure.py`](measure.py) loads the pinned Qwen3-0.6B layer-0 fixture and original BF16 Q/K/V/O and Q/K RMSNorm gamma. It reshapes the existing 2,048 training and 1,024 validation input positions into their 256-token source windows, using the **first two train windows and first two validation windows**, with no parameter tuning on validation. It independently decodes the existing 8,704-byte affine Q4 and 8,128-byte signed-carrier96 files from their stored codes/scales, replaces *only* original Q rows 0:128 × input columns 0:128 and retains all other Q coefficients. It then replays BF16-rounded complete 1,024-input head-0 Q, original K/V, per-head RMSNorm, RoPE, causal softmax, value mixing, and original O projection of that head's contribution. This preserves the real input continuations and visible causal keys; it does not confuse the 128-input subprojection with the complete Q head. The post-O squared-error denominator is the **original head-0 contribution**, not the entire 1,024-dimensional full-model attention output. Scores are FP32 Torch approximations to the runtime BF16/SDPA path, not byte-identical GPU execution.

| Held local head-0 reader | Static submatrix payload | Head-0 raw Q relative sq. | Head-0 attention KL | Head-0 post-O relative sq. |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original BF16 submatrix | 32,768 B | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Exported affine Q4 | **8,704 B** | .001745 | **.001805** | **.002262** |
| Exported signed-carrier96 | 8,128 B | .035914 | .071239 | .096375 |
| Q4 + free paired Q/K sign relabeling | **8,704 B** | **1.020177** | **.001805** | **.002262** |
| Signed-carrier96 + paid K correction | **8,384 B** | .035914 (same Q) | .066699 | .085788 |

The Q4 path reads 16,384 coefficient contributions per query. Signed-carrier96 reads two dense 128×96 factors, **24,576** coefficient contributions per query before gains and scales. The K correction additionally uses 64 complex multiplications (four real products and two adds each) **per key token**; it is not a same-work competitor. The original BF16 submatrix is a strong direct-coefficient accuracy control at larger bytes. A literal direct table of all four windows' FP16 causal scores would require 4×(256×257/2)×2 = **263,168 bytes** before its addressing and cannot represent unseen histories, so it is not passed off as a viable cheaper model control.

[`joint.py`](joint.py) learns one **static** complex coefficient per RoPE pair for the shared K head: `k'_x=αk_x−βk_y`, `k'_y=βk_x+αk_y`. This commutes with RoPE and changes only the live K continuation, not per-history cached tables. On the two train windows it minimizes the teacher-probability-weighted, row-centered logit quadratic with a declared `10⁻⁴ × mean Gram diagonal` ridge toward identity. It rounds all 128 real coefficients to FP16 and writes the **256-byte** [`key-correction.bin`](key-correction.bin), then decodes those saved bytes for both panels; its SHA256 is in [`results.json`](results.json). No validation target enters fitting. The same script also evaluates a **free-real coefficient projection** for this fixed signed Q image and 64-complex-dimensional K family. Per visible causal row, subtract the teacher-probability-weighted mean of both target scores and 128 pairwise Q/K score features; accumulate `G=ΣFᵀ diag(p) F`, `b=ΣFᵀ diag(p)y`, and `c=Σyᵀ diag(p)y`. The unrestricted least-squares residual is `c−bᵀG⁻¹b`. On the two training windows, G has numerical rank 128 (minimum eigenvalue 1.7435; relative normal-equation residual `3.67e−14`). This gives **43.0144** summed weighted centered-score loss, versus **1.38793** for the paid Q4 image evaluated on the exact same teacher and rows: a **30.99×** conditional floor. Both losses use the unnormalized sum over all visible causal rows. This is an FP64 calculation from FP32/BF16-rounded fixture operations, **not an exact rational certificate** and **not** a KL or post-O lower bound; softmax can attenuate residual score errors. It does reject the claim that this particular signed Q image merely needs a better optimizer within the same low-work 64-complex K-correction family to match Q4's *weighted score response*. The ridge-fitted serialized candidate is an optimization heuristic for *attention KL*, not a globally optimal joint Q/K search. Applying it to the GQA-shared key also perturbs Q head 1. Held GQA-pair mean KL rises to **.038332** versus matched Q4's **.000902**; the combined two-head O response error is **.012912** versus Q4's **.000220**. These are measured on both affected heads, not a claim about every other unchanged model head or model NLL.

RMSNorm and causal score geometry explain why a direct Euclidean Q rejection is **not** generally transferable to attention. Conversely, this particular signed image remains bad through the live observer, even with one cheap joint key family. A broad relaxed response floor in raw Q coordinates cannot be reused as an attention impossibility theorem: the paired sign gauge is already a counterexample. A genuinely universal complete-consumer floor would need to quantify over priced K changes, both GQA query heads, the finite reachable histories and the softmax/value endpoint. This study does not have that floor.

## Reproduction and limits

```sh
cd /path/to/workspace/projects/kelana
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 /path/to/workspace/data/fish-s2-pro/venv/bin/python research/isa-quantization/attention-consumer/measure.py
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 /path/to/workspace/data/fish-s2-pro/venv/bin/python research/isa-quantization/attention-consumer/joint.py
lake env lean Kelana/AttentionConsumer.lean
```

Each CPU command is sub-minute. `measure.py` regenerates the baseline result; run `joint.py` afterward to add the paid correction. The fixture and model remain with their established owners; no large arrays or copied model weights are committed here. Both response panels are bounded, previously studied source windows, not future-history guarantees. The paired sign is a representation symmetry but does not make a cheaper model by itself; its changed Q Euclidean error is deliberately large. The signed image loses on both accuracy and coefficient-work count to the scalar Q4 control here, although its static bytes are lower. No GPU timing or whole-model replacement follows.
