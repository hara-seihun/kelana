# Comparable-size decision audit of the ISA catalogue

the project lead's correction changes the acceptance question: a lower-byte quantizer is **expected** to have higher loss than Q4. The goal is a better size/quality/work frontier than credible quantizers **at comparable size**, not a mandate that a Q2/Q3-sized family beat the loss of a larger Q4 image. A local Q4 image remains a useful diagnostic, and a theorem about failing its exact loss threshold remains true. Neither makes that threshold a family-termination rule. A different, independently stated absolute accuracy requirement could exclude a family; none of the audited real ISA studies establishes such a requirement.

[`audit.json`](audit.json) covers **all 52** entries of `research/catalogue/isa-quantization.json` at canonical Kelana commit `b6cbc42a82a7f091ec1fde29524f364c196f87ee`. It records the catalogue SHA-256, every ID/category, the primary report path and its SHA-256, classification, rationale, concrete proposed change and exact field replacements. The parent accepted all nine changes (34 exact field replacements), preserving newer entries and the measured/theorem evidence. Eight `strictly-bad` entries became `promising` with narrower titles and next actions; the umbrella priority was corrected. The original assessment and replacements remain in `audit.json` as ordinary provenance. One-shot snapshot/application scripts were removed after adoption; the current catalogue and primary reports are the maintained owners, not a second migration workflow.

## Findings and decision distinctions

- **Nine concrete catalogue corrections:** eight narrowly Q4-gated negative-family narratives and the umbrella programme's Q4-gated priority language. They have exact replacement `title/category/summary/next` (or umbrella `limitation/next`) in the machine-readable receipt. `promising` in a replacement means **the comparable-size research question remains open**, not that its failed selected image won.
- **Forty-three scoped entries to retain:** this includes three of the eleven `strictly-bad` items: `function-kernel` has a direct producer-law moment mismatch independent of Q4; `table-diversity` has a genuinely equal-tile-byte uniform control and a smaller, much more accurate stored polynomial; `nonlinear-response-bank` has a directly observed train-to-held fitting/transfer failure for its selected procedure. Each negative remains restricted to that law, local table domain, or selected fit, respectively. The 40 other retained `promising` entries contain mathematics, finite grammars, positives, or scoped Q4 controls without a real-family Q4-only termination claim; the original forty-first is the umbrella entry proposed for correction.
- **Same-byte/local controls are valid, not SOTA dominance:** the sparse-circuit image is 8,672 B versus a local Q4 at 8,704 B; co-designed and frozen-code corrections have the same paid bytes; sign-embedded transported norms match Q4's 8,704 static bytes; square and uniform tables store the same 64 B/tile. These establish the stated *local candidate comparisons*. They do not silently equalize reader code, native work, cache/prepared residency, complete-model BPW, or language loss. A smaller static polynomial on a designed table grid is not yet measured complete paid-image/native dominance.
- **Exact mathematical certificates remain exact at their threshold:** the signed-pair PSD/matching dual gives a train response floor `.0051768145 > .0051736144` for the 8,704-B Q4 comparator; the signed dense-readout rank floor and one-chart affine floor have their own specified captured-panel contracts. The first excludes that pair family **from attaining that particular Q4 local error**, not from occupying a useful ~6,788-B rate/quality point. The dense rank screen's numerical floor and the one-chart pooled QR floor similarly cannot be promoted to absolute target-quality or full-consumer/SOTA rejection. Reflection parity remains a theorem on its symmetric stress domain, not a reachability or byte-frontier verdict. The weighted-pair diagonal-certificate *ceiling* is about a proof method, never a paid programme.
- **No named external/SOTA acceptance is present in these 52 entries.** A complete-model Q4 diagnostic at 4.251 BPW and a ternary model result at 1.727 BPW are cited by `producer-screen`, but neither gives a credible size-matched **local** response/control or a matched whole-model candidate comparison. No entry establishes real-family exclusion against an externally mandated target error; no entry demonstrates full paid-image accuracy-and-work dominance by an alternative of no greater size on the deployment boundary. Marking these explicitly false prevents a local Q4 threshold from being renamed a SOTA result.

The highest-impact reversals are `input-programs` (preserve its exact certificate but reopen the smaller signed-pair frontier), `producer-screen` (dense rank constraints versus a larger Q4 do not exclude all useful carrier rates), `response-atlas` (the single-affine floor is a mathematical finite-panel fact, not a no-greater-byte chart competitor), and `antipodal-gated-algebra` (odd-map obligation survives without condemning joint even/odd representations). `coded-shared-correction` and `joint-residual-reader` retain useful within-family co-design/correction gains. All eleven original negative labels and every primary README were examined; no experiment was rerun.

The new [matched-rate controls](../matched-rate-frontier/README.md) make the changed conclusion concrete: the initial equal-byte GPTQ-style controls at 6,848/7,104 bytes did not cross the universal pair floors. A full-covariance scale/origin refit then lowered the 7,104-byte scalar train error to .005064387, below the .005188093 affine-family certificate. That is now a valid equal-byte captured-train-error exclusion; the 6,848-byte linear family remains open against its .005975855 control. Neither is a SOTA or joint work/quality claim. Integrating a strong structured quantizer remains the explicit next step.
