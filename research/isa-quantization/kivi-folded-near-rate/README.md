# Frozen near-rate composition: metric K or extra recent folded V?

**Complete-source outcome.** On the original Qwen3-0.6B layer-0 two-query-head/one-KV-head, after the *same fixed physical folded-V/O weight replacement*, two already-frozen causal cache programs differ by 16 counted bytes at the 256-token peak. **A** uses the original [train-derived rank8-plus-diagonal metric K-code assignment](../kivi-response-metric/) with folded V, original R32; **B** uses original KIVI K code and the *same* folded V source/grid with R45 retention. Inspected-held full GQA-pair post-O relative squared error is **A .00012232251 at 54,704 B** versus **B .00012781412 at 54,688 B**. A reduces held pair SSE by **4.30%** against B and attention KL `.00413072/.00119658 → .00302252/.00091559` (heads 0/1), at 16 more bytes for **one sequence** *and* extra offline model-specific preparation and rank8 K-flush work. Train pair reverses: **B .00011543380 versus A .00012116856** (B 4.73% less SSE). A does not dominate per-head O: on held head0, A `.00094504` versus B `.00090795` relative squared. This is a useful quality/size/work tradeoff, not an unqualified frontier win, native speedup, new code/permutation fit, full-model result or served deployment.

## Fixed donors and paid source identity

No new projection or quantizer is fitted. Both arms use the one stable train-RMS V-channel order and corresponding **physical** 128×1024 BF16 V-row and both-head 1024×256 BF16 O-column replacement images in [`../kivi-v-channel-fold/`](../kivi-v-channel-fold/): V SHA256 `54b649480a54f6ee8f02e141590747a795af7b090beed8a555544cf06bd01f23` (262,144 B), O SHA256 `ec728d7131f96389d2dac5124afe3a0a520a4579a7d65d740c54d4e67d0ae28e` (524,288 B). They replace exactly 786,432 B of common original static V/O source, rather than adding another table. The permutation and RMS JSON are offline provenance, not resident metadata; no online gather. Original Q, K, RoPE, Q/K norm, two shared-K/V query heads, value projection BF16, 1,024 output columns, causal score/softmax and original teacher remain fixed. The folded donor was frozen in commit `447a40aa3155d2ca2a6874fa92b97b94bca11007`; the primary source prior-art scope in its README refers to SKVQ-like channel reordering rather than a novel attention map.

A takes each unchanged 2,560-B per-channel FP16-grid **metric-assigned key slab** byte for byte from frozen [`../kivi-response-metric/`](../kivi-response-metric/) commit `c31c930c0bfa829af5d7af03a9690da87b9f2008` at its original causal flush, together with its paid **2,304-B model-specific FP16 U128×8/D128** asset SHA256 `2d4837028c2e04fb61b3f554eb409b4ff450374634b54febabe451cd846cadc9`. Its key FP16 affine fields are byte-identical to original KIVI; original K nibble assignments are the only changed K state. A takes each already-built 80-B folded V-token event from the folded donor at the donor's original R32 flush; recent folded BF16 V tokens are inserted from the physical folded V source. B instead takes each original K payload byte for byte from the folded donor, and each *same folded* V-token payload from that donor **13 query times later**: at query time `t`, token `t−46` was already available and its source-only FP16 grid/code does not depend on the intervening future tokens. This is the fixed [R45 retention control](../kivi-v45-control/) applied to the folded source, with no new residual threshold selection. The donor V event is compared against the present source token's BF16 words, min/max, FP16 fields and nibble codes. No changed K code, V grouping, bitwidth, field dtype, head split, teacher, train/held membership or event schedule was selected after observing A/B output.

[`compose.py`](compose.py) constructs each actual image/event log using these frozen donor bytes, checking full donor-log SHA, original-versus-folded K identity, metric versus original K FP16-field identity and folded source/metric image hashes; its per-window A/B manifests pin **all** donor image/log SHA values, composed image/log hashes, exact state sizes and eight K plus 224/211 V chronological events. Each `PANEL-WINDOW-ARM-prefixes.json` pins the **256 pre-query-flush paid-state hashes**, not a future final-token reconstruction. [`replay.py`](replay.py) independently re-reads only the arriving original/folded BF16 token source and *the actual composed events when their causal flush occurs*, checking all 256 preflush hashes, both final images, every K event against the correct donor, all V events against the folded donor and source FP16 grid/nibble oracle, then computes both complete Q score rows, stable softmax, all quantized/recent V reductions and the original full BF16 O readout. It checks that A's per-head attention KL matches frozen metric-K and B's matches frozen folded-original-K on **all eight train and four inspected-held windows**. The source uses the same original capture and BF16 checkpoint provenance as the owners. Event logs and 256 hashes are receipts, **not retained cache state**. [`aggregate.py`](aggregate.py) requires all 24 independent replay receipts, aggregates actual SSE and teacher-square numerators before division and names the standalone donor controls in [`results.json`](results.json).

## Bytes and execution obligations

At prefix256 **before** query flush both share original KIVI source weights (1,573,376 B static two-Q/K/V/O/gamma group, with 786,432 B of V/O bytes replaced) and original Q/K projection/norm/RoPE and K/V producer operations. Their actual cache states are:

| State | Packed K slabs | BF16 recent K | Packed V tokens | BF16 recent V | Group-specific metric | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A R32, 7×2,560-B K slabs / 223×80-B V tokens | 17,920 | 8,192 (32) | 17,840 | 8,448 (33) | **2,304** | **54,704 B** |
| B R45, same K / 210×80-B V tokens | 17,920 | 8,192 (32) | 16,800 | 11,776 (46) | 0 | **54,688 B** |
| A after t256 flush | 20,480 | 0 | 17,920 | 8,192 (32) | 2,304 | **48,896 B** |
| B after t256 flush | 20,480 | 0 | 16,880 | 11,520 (45) | 0 | **48,880 B** |

A requires frozen train-only 4,096 Q-head source queries to build covariance/eigenvectors once per source group and persistent U/D FP16 image. At **each eight key-chunk flushes**, in addition to original 32×128 min/max, original nearest-code assignment and FP16 field writes, it initializes rank8 state (`32×128×8` products) and performs one forward 128-coordinate sweep over 32 key tokens with rank8 gradient and incremental state update (two more `32×128×8` product/update sets, including one scalar 16-level nearest-code decision per source element). The previously checked key-image metric decreases and finite-grid derivation are owned by [`../kivi-response-metric/`](../kivi-response-metric/); this study does not repeat fitting/theorem. B retains the ordinary KIVI key flush and instead delays 13 original-format V flushes: at t256 it retains 13 additional BF16 V vectors instead of 13 packed V records, with extra recent-byte traffic/capacity and 13×128 BF16 rather than dequantized-nibble V contributions *per query/head* at peak. Both still compute two complete causal scores, softmax, 128D V mixture per head and original 1,024×256 BF16 O matrix product (262,144 O multiply/add terms). V coordinate folding costs offline 2,048 train source V projections, RMS/sort, and one physical 786,432-B replacement for both arms; its online projected V/O shapes and no-gather work are shared. The common 2-B position counter is omitted from both totals. Across **N simultaneous independent sequences** at this same t256 high-water for one model group, A's immutable FP16 U/D asset is **shared once**: `52,400×N + 2,304 B`, while B's extra recent V lives **per sequence**: `54,688×N B`. At N=1 A is 16 B larger; already at N=2 A is **107,104 B versus B 109,376 B**. This is an analytical serialized/live-state crossover, **not** a measured native-memory or throughput result: any prepared metric copies, per-query register/LDS state, temporary decoded cache, and the metric sweep work **repeated for each sequence** must be priced separately. The original CPU metric-assignment producer uses FP64 working e/s/coefficients derived from the paid FP16 metric; that temporary preparation is not included in 2,304 B. CPU acceptance here expands packed 256×128 K/V to up to 262,144 B transient FP32 scratch per query, not a claimed 55-KB total process. **No GPU launch, native compilation, latency measurement or serving adoption**; existing packed reader work ledgers remain the honest native control.
