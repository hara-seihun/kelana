# Original-G32 arbitrary-joint rounding: source-uniform frame floor

This is one frozen source-only certificate rule and one fixed-readout risk screen. It applies to **any joint law of all 32 coordinates inside each original V G32**, with the same clipped unbiased coordinate means and original actual decoded four-level grids. Different groups/events are independent, and the two Q heads of a KV head read the same draw. It neither constructs an encoder nor changes K2 probabilities, original fields, timing, O, teacher, source, or the mean-output risk. Laws with larger marginal variance (including nonadjacent four-level choices) are included. It says nothing about coupling **across** groups/events, changing means, or nonlinear continuation.

For one layer, KV head and original G32, `B` is the actual 1024×64 matrix of original O columns: first the 32 columns of Q head `2h`, then those of Q head `2h+1`. Its exact integer Gram `G=BᵀB` uses unit `2⁻⁷⁰`; `D=diag(G)` is strictly positive in all 64 observed groups. [`source.py`](source.py) takes one FP64 `eigvalsh` of `D⁻¹ᐟ² G D⁻¹ᐟ²` and freezes `λ=floor(4096 λ_min)/4096`. It then certifies **independently in exact arithmetic**, without eigenvalue backoff, that `4096G−(4096λ)D` is positive definite: Sage rational block LDLᵀ has 64 positive one-by-one pivots. For each group, [`cert-L-H.json.gz`](cert-0-0.json.gz) contains the exact Gram upper triangle, diagonal, numerator, eigvalsh receipt, exact rational positive pivots and permutation. All 64 certificates succeeded at the frozen rule. The smallest/largest numerators are **546/2534**; all 64 groups contain no zero source words, explicitly counted. The exact source matrices are the two SHA-pinned original BF16 O files (`803995a9…` layer 0; `677e1de4…` layer 1), not fitted quantizer weights.

The [finite rational frame/covariance foundation](../value-rounding-risk/GROUP_FLOOR.md) derives factor positivity, arbitrary-joint risk pushforward, shared-head lifting and cross-group additivity in Lean. For an arbitrary centered 32-vector `e` and real (in particular nonnegative attention) masses `u,v`, exact positive definiteness implies

`||u O[L,q0,G] e + v O[L,q1,G] e||² ≥ λ Σⱼ (u² G[j,j] + v² G[32+j,32+j]) eⱼ² · 2⁻⁷⁰`.

The scalar `2⁻⁷⁰` is suppressed if `G` denotes its physical rather than integer Gram. Taking expectations over any joint 32-coordinate law uses only its marginal variances; by the two-point adjacent-grid variance minimum at fixed clipped mean, these are at least `f(1−f)(b−a)²`. Independence between **different** groups/events removes their cross-covariances in the centered sum. The unchanged mean bias adds back separately. Thus the result is a law-uniform output-SSE lower bound, not a covariance model or an empirical seed result.

[`risk.py`](risk.py) reads the pinned independent-rounding owner without rescoring its means or controls. It rebuilds only original-V33 decoded levels/adjacent marginal variance and the same original-K2 pre-query FP32 probabilities, with K32 after-query chronology. Event/head coefficients `C0,C1` are the sums over the four G32s of `λ·diag(Gq0/q1)·adjacent_variance`, stored for each of 224 aged tokens and eight KV heads in every panel-window receipt. Each query adds `Σaged,h (p0² C0+p1² C1)` to the pinned mean-bias SSE. [`summary.json`](summary.json) SHA-pins all 16 source certificates, all 16 original owner receipts, donor/source identities, event logs/fields, and all new per-query receipts.

The bound lies **below**, not above, deterministic on all three pooled panels, despite 188 train and 75 validation individual queries whose floor exceeds their own deterministic SSE. It does not rule out an improved arbitrary G32 joint law, nor does it promise that such a law exists. The prior independent law's variance is 93.612750/50.809169/0.337181, so the source-uniform frame bound is genuinely weaker than independence while covering a strictly broader set of within-group couplings.

Reproduce one source certificate at a time (each bounded): `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 sage -python source.py L H` for `L=0,1`, `H=0..7`. [`verify.py`](verify.py) independently reconstructs the integer Gram from original BF16 bytes, checks all 2,080 exact upper-triangle entries, recomputes exact rational LDLᵀ and explicitly checks its factor-congruence identity, stored permutation/pivots, positivity, and a direct 1024-row mixed-Q projection against the Gram and bound: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 sage -python verify.py L H G`, one G32 per bounded call. Representative independent congruence/projection checks completed for `(0,0,0)`, `(0,0,3)` (nontrivial permutation) and `(1,5,1)`; generation itself performed exact positivity on **all** groups. Risk replay: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 /path/to/workspace/data/fish-s2-pro/venv/bin/python risk.py PANEL WINDOW` for train `0..7`, validation `0..3`, held `0..3`; then `python aggregate.py`. No sample, GPU, new quantizer or forward call occurs.

The ~3.5 MB compressed rational certificates are offline source-analysis evidence, **not** runtime cache. No online map, metadata field or quantizer is proposed or priced. Source study builds 64 exact 1024×64 Grams and 64 exact 64×64 rational LDLᵀ factorizations once per O revision; risk replay consists of marginal-grid arithmetic and fixed-attention contractions. Runtime state remains unchanged for the actual prior KIVI2 comparator; an arbitrary alternative joint sampler has no asserted runtime cost or benefit.
