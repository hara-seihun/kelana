**Nonlinear source structure is not optional, but source channels are.** Selected-channel banks fail transfer, the explicit Gaussian law fails moments, and direct affine charts miss curvature. **Global labels and causal state are distinct constraints.** Independently optimized binary-tree nodes can each fit with two labels while the whole categorical law requires many. The all-C causal and right-suffix certificates address that second gap. **Search machinery is available.** Exact interaction elimination, balanced signed cuts, conditioning and cheap factor floors preserve the finite paid frontier. Stronger bounds are not always faster.
