The next completed studies sharpen what needs to change. [Cross-pair covariance](pair-covariance/README.md) proves a whole-family obstruction to the diagonal method: with one global soft input direction, it pays that direction once per pair, whereas only one independent lost direction can use it. The true weighted-pair error can exceed the best diagonal certificate by an arbitrarily large factor. The tested rank-one corrections did not cross the threshold, but the subsequent [48-mode shared dual](full-pair-covariance/README.md) does: .0057530035 with exact PSD/edge-cone/matching checks. These first resolved Q4-accuracy questions; only the later matched-rate comparison establishes a no-larger local accuracy control for one rate. General finite rational covariance/matching assembly is now Lean-proved, with real PSD/ratio bridges explicit.

[Attention continuation](attention-consumer/README.md) makes the observation boundary concrete. A paired Q/K sign transformation increases raw-Q error dramatically with exactly unchanged attention scores; Euclidean Q rejection is not a universal attention rejection. [Direct affine charts](response-atlas/README.md) discard source hidden channels completely, but their .805 validation RMS loses to the decoded all-three-matrix Q4 control's .163. [SiLU reflection](antipodal-gated-algebra/README.md) exactly contracts the even part of the MLP to a quadratic map; its odd continuation remains large and strongly cancels it. These results argue for a jointly approximated nonlinear base and correction, not another increase in affine or finite-sample interpolation capacity. They do not show that source channels must be preserved.

The parent's [categorical tree calculus](categorical-tree/README.md) separates exact loss decomposition from legal state sharing. Bernoulli node floors add through an exact KL chain rule, and joining node groups strengthens them. But for every `2≤C<V`, positive categorical laws can make the best independent-node tree floor arbitrarily small relative to the true C-label optimum. These are complementary dependencies: **executable coordinate realization, nonlinear cancellation, common labels and causal histories**. ## A more precise mathematics of continuation

[Continuation contexts](continuation-context/README.md) characterizes the exact universal reduction: every legal suffix of a discarded prefix must have a same-whole-response, no-more-costly realization from its representative. Lean proves both frontier preservation and its converse for all terminal losses and independent resource budgets. Numeric-state equality is sufficient but not necessary. An exposed register or a future shared-asset reuse can prevent compression. Full residual enumeration is a characterization, not automatically a fast algorithm.

[Shared frontiers](shared-frontier/README.md) shows why costs must remain vectors with asset identities. A paid dictionary wins across three regions although it loses on every isolated tile. Another nondominated executable point loses under every **nonzero** nonnegative linear weighting. A weighted amortization criterion cannot recover every indivisible deployment choice.

[Closure through continuation](closure-continuation/README.md) replaces a polynomial-degree intuition with a function-algebra statement. Every translation-invariant pointwise algebra on binary inputs is the Walsh algebra of a linear quotient. It can carry XOR updates and arbitrary pointwise nonlinearities without decoding source gates. For a product consumer, the extra squared error of multiplying fitted branch tables rather than fitting the product table directly is exactly the squared conditional covariance of their residuals. Structured witnesses benefit; unstructured controls reverse the ranking. The conditional-projection algebraic identities have Lean proofs; the complete Fourier characterization and squared-norm statement retain their distinct proof status.

[Behavioral continuation](behavioral-continuation/README.md) provides a nonlocal objective without pretending current logits determine future state. A finite teacher/candidate product machine follows the same history with **each program's own transition**. Its occupancy gives sequence KL. At fixed candidate transitions, optimal unconstrained emissions are weighted centroids and the remaining irreducible loss is conditional mutual information. A rational two-state example has zero aligned teacher-forced emission error yet positive sequence KL. This is an admissible floor for a fixed transition structure; the transition-envelope and state-budget studies above supply further arguments for incomplete transitions and relaxed state capacities.

These results motivated the compact interacting-continuation work above. The [continuing plan](ORCHESTRATION.md#next-bounded-decisions) records the current parent-led mathematics and independent transfer questions.

## What the foundational round established

The strongest conclusion is that **the approximation should target a nearby executable map**. Several examples improve when source coefficients or individual operations cease to be the objects being preserved. The best evidence for this is not a planted exact ternary matrix. It is the gap between forcing individual gates into a convenient family and directly approximating the complete response of that family.

## Search is the first thing to improve

The parent [projection experiment](projection-bounds/README.md) gives an instruction structure more freedom than its paid fields permit. It computes the best response in that larger family, then rejects the structure when even that optimistic response loses. A second bound accounts for the discrete coefficient grid without enumerating every code.

On twelve generic rational teachers, the exact packed assignments scored fall from 6,190,380 to 191,607. Every optimum and deterministic tie winner matches the exhaustive oracle. This is a 32.3-fold reduction in assignment evaluations, not an inference speedup or a wall-time ratio. The experiment still pays for 1,260 small exact envelope calculations.

The other search studies supply different reductions:

- [Indexed meet-in-the-middle](search-factorization/README.md) splits a whole program at its complete live machine state. One eight-operation optimum needs 972 half-search semantic transitions and 1,925 indexed intersections, versus 283,878 transitions for whole-map breadth-first search. Shared static bits must agree at the join.
- [Priced symmetries](search-symmetries/README.md) identify programs only when the quantizer, instructions, observer and costs all transform together. A lane swap halves 4,662 labeled choices. Equal fibers alone are insufficient.
- [Stateful observation](stateful-maps/README.md) reduces a two-state linear recurrence to observable invariants. This removes search dimensions without pretending that hidden state is irrelevant to every consumer.

These reductions have separate proofs. Their savings cannot be multiplied together from these experiments; the combined round above directly tests that issue and finds redundant work.

### A useful mathematical organization

Let the candidate be `O(P(z,E(x)))`. Keep a frontier in complete behavioral distortion, static bytes, live resources and measured execution cost. Effective BPW is total paid model-specific bytes times eight divided by the original unique parameter count. It need not imply one code for each original weight.

A search node needs more than a current output vector. It needs the complete reachable machine state, any remaining instruction restrictions, shared static choices, live consumers and the set of already-paid code paths or tables. Two equal current outputs can have different legal completions. Shared code is charged by set union, so dropping those identities can also corrupt future byte costs.

For a candidate structure with responses contained in `A theta`, the rational certificate

```
f = A a + r,       A^T W r = 0
```

proves the complete-response squared-error floor `r^T W r`. For independent columns, with `G=A^T W A` and legal coefficient sets `S_j`, a stronger floor is

```
r^T W r + max_j distance(a_j,S_j)^2 / (G^-1)[j,j].
```

The maximum is deliberate. Adding those coordinate penalties is generally unsound because they can count the same correlated displacement more than once. The projection report includes an exact counterexample.

For nonlinear parameter families, a safe larger response set or a partition into structural cases is needed. A tangent approximation is not automatically a lower bound. The same containment obligation applies to native rounding and overflow. This is how a large search can remain exact where it claims exactness.

## Where quantization creates executable structure

[Encoded continuations](encoded-continuations/README.md) gives the clearest obstruction. Two maps are each one XOR under their own labels, yet cannot share any exact two-XOR labeling because the maps do not commute. Jointly changing both maps reduces one-step squared error from 64 to 32 under a shared label. Observing both two-step orders changes the best choice again. A local quantizer cannot decide the interface on behalf of those future consumers.

[Instruction cells](instruction-cells/README.md) provides a less planted positive. Squaring an unsigned five-bit input and shifting chooses eight nonuniform cells with two assembled gfx1151 instructions. With the same paid FP16 response table, it beats a searched affine family on 27 of 32 random SiLU-response teachers. It loses on every linear and unstructured control. The useful variable is the geometry induced by the instruction, not a scalar alphabet chosen beforehand.

[Nonlinear closure](nonlinear-closure/README.md) separates two routes to a simple map. On six binary inputs, restricted SiLU gates make an entire region cubic. Snapping perturbed source gates to satisfy the restriction gives .17779 relative RMS; directly approximating the complete cubic response gives .00689 at the same 176 bytes. On unrestricted Gaussian gates, the corresponding errors are .52407 and .04539. Approximate closure of the observed map is much less restrictive than exact closure of its source gates. The advantage disappears on continuous inputs, so the producer domain is part of the construction.

[Carry boundaries](carry-boundaries/README.md) shows another kind of structure. Editing one response-table cell from 128 to 127 removes a byte correction with error 1/8; every integer coefficient edit that makes that example byte-safe has error at least one. But an exact biased label accepted by the next consumer erases the lossy advantage. The continuation decides whether the approximation buys anything.

These are different ways for quantization to change the program. None requires the answer to retain a separate scale and ternary block.

## What the stronger controls ruled out

Several attractive stories became narrower after comparison:

- A magnitude ladder makes certain FP16 operand bits cheap to construct, but loses on most unstructured rows to freely fitted scalar grids.
- Whole-K scale coherence removes an accumulator bank in a declared schedule. A same-byte sign-magnitude alphabet beats the ternary witness, and activation scales must also be coherent. The register model alone does not pick a winning quantizer.
- Shared output rows win near rank one and fail on noncollinear geometry. Injectivity does not rescue a cheap linear endpoint; an arbitrary decoder has its own storage and execution cost.
- Complementary route codes expose a compact contrast representation, but a competent conventional fused consumer already uses the same algebra.
- The compiled template reader really reduces total description bytes on cyclic tiles after paying its larger code. Those tiles are constructed; the result does not establish trained-weight prevalence.

These negatives should shape the search menus. They do not reject the larger joint-search problem.

## How the proposed experiments resolved

The first round proposed combining the search reductions on one finite, fully specified machine grammar. [Combined search](combined-search/README.md) now does this and measures each reduction separately; its independent-cell elimination is the stronger next result. The original contract was: Let input encoding, model-specific fields, instruction sequence and final observation vary. Keep a conventional packed scalar reader and a direct table in the same paid comparison. Use complete tiny domains and generic nonlinear targets, with one live continuation so a cheap intermediate label cannot conceal an expensive boundary. A brute-force oracle remains feasible at the smallest size.

The decisive measurement was frontier preservation together with actual search time and memory. All reductions preserve the frontier, but fewer scored assignments do not imply less total work. Projection plus symmetry beats the all-three combination in this grammar.

Second, the proposed full input-to-output lowering is complete in [native cells](cell-native/README.md). It pays table access and a live consumer and includes the optimized affine and direct-table readers. The outcome is a storage/accuracy distinction with no native speed win on hot tables.

Third, [producer screening](producer-screen/README.md) uses existing real model states rather than training another model. It measures response-space floors and exact captured-rank obstructions, then follows a low-bit readout survivor through an actual signed-input export. The full attention continuation remains outside that subprojection result. In further transfer work, measure the exact distinctions required by the live consumer, the response-space floor for each family, and the producer-domain violations. A family whose optimistic floor already fails needs no expensive code fitting. A family that survives gets an actual packed export and held consumer evaluation before whole-model replacement.

[Observation loss](observation-loss/README.md) supplies exact sparse softmax edit scores for that later stage. Teacher KL, gold-token loss and future-state behavior remain separate quantities. The whole-model acceptance must evaluate the candidate's own state on the same history; a small current logit error is not a certificate for what happens next.
