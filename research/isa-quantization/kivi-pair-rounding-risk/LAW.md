# Frozen source-only pair-coupled rounding law

The assignment fixed this law before any score was computed. It is one ideal probability law, not a sampled seed search or a fitted coupling. Keep the original K2/V2 G32 records (two-bit codes and FP16 fields), K/recent state, event timing, O and teacher. For each flushed V coordinate, compute the four decoded levels using separate FP32 multiply/add, choose the adjacent levels bracketing its BF16 source value, and clip outside the range, exactly as in the pinned independent law. Let lower endpoint `a`, gap `d=b-a`, and upper probability `p=(source-a)/d` for a distinct bracket; at clipped/exact endpoints set `p=d=0` or the corresponding degenerate endpoint. The clipped mean is unchanged. Use the layer-specific lexicographically selected source-only perfect matching within each original G32 group, without rematching or reordering.

For paired coordinates `i,j`, upper marginals `p,q` and the source sign `+` or `-`, set `t=max(0,p+q-1)` for `+` (antithetic), or `t=min(p,q)` for `-` (comonotonic). The `(lower,lower)`, `(upper,lower)`, `(lower,upper)`, `(upper,upper)` joint weights are `1-p-q+t, p-t, q-t, t`. One fresh uniform per pair/group/token/KV head samples this four-way distribution; draws are independent between pairs and events. Both Q heads consuming the KV head see the **same** V realization. All the source data are available at the V flush; no future query/token is consulted. No choice changes after scores.

The source O tensors and map are separate per layer. For a KV head `h` and coordinate `d`, columns `(2h)*128+d` and `(2h+1)*128+d` belong to that **same** layer. Layer 0 and layer 1 maps occupy independent 1,568-byte slices of the 3,136-byte static map. The superseded cross-layer interpretation must never be used for this outcome.

For attention masses `u,v>=0`, the exact complete two-Q-head coefficient for pair `i,j` is `(A*u²+B*u*v+D*v²)*2^-70`, where the source coefficients are exact integers from the same O layer. The source certificate says this quadratic is nonnegative for `+` and nonpositive for `-` on the full nonnegative quadrant. The pair covariance is `(t-p*q)*d_i*d_j`, nonpositive for `+` and nonnegative for `-` because actual decoded gaps are nonnegative. Hence the exact variance difference from independent rounding is `2*covariance*coefficient <=0`, even with unequal gaps, zero attention, clipped endpoints, and arbitrary joint marginals in `[0,1]`. The mean-output SSE and all independent coordinate variance terms are unchanged. This theorem compares **expectations against the independent law**, not against biased deterministic nearest rounding.
