# Physical rate of the longer fresh-text ternary recovery

The selected `fresh-duration16` Qwen3-0.6B complete image improves a newly frozen, untouched eight-window / 2,040-prediction held panel from **4.343739 to 4.330215 NLL** against its `fresh-scale8` predecessor (paired improvement **.013524 ± .002129 nats**, 8/8 windows favorable). [The training and evaluation report](README.md) fixes the two images, text partition and complete-model observation. This result prices the *new* scales instead of assuming that an unchanged 1.727086 raw BPW implies unchanged physical rate.

The existing 256-row, group-major, random-addressable FP16 scale-page codec was run independently on the two complete images. Each page stores a uint16 minimum, uint8 delta width, bit-packed unsigned deltas, and a paid uint32 offset; all other packed matrix fields and norms are copied unchanged. The decoder checked **every one of 4,656,128 scale words** by page offset against the source FP16 bit pattern in each image, including tail pages. Both images contain 197 matrices and 18,192 pages.

| Complete image | Physical bytes | Physical BPW | Held NLL on the same new panel |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `fresh-scale8` | 125,953,437 | 1.690508567 | 4.343739 |
| `fresh-duration16` | **125,953,533** | **1.690509855** | **4.330215** |

The improvement costs **96 physical bytes / 0.000001288 BPW**, only 0.0000762% of the source's paid image. Both retain the 128,678,649-byte raw payload, identical trits/signs/shapes/norms, and 4,656,128 scale words; 2,775,304 scale words changed. The paid delta is exactly in the scale streams (6,587,044 → 6,587,140 bytes), not uncharged metadata. Thirteen matrices change encoded size; page-width crossings raise the net by three 256-row width increments (32 bytes each). This establishes an improved **complete-model quality/paid-rate** point, not an exactly equal physical-rate point and not a compressed-runtime gain. Bitwise decoding preserves each image's own expanded-BF16 quality observation. BF16 and calibrated Q4 remain much better language models on their separately measured panels.
