# Complete ternary scale bitplane pages do not repay their decoder

The frozen Qwen3-0.6B `fresh-duration32` complete image has 197 matrices and 4,656,128 FP16 scale words. Its 256-output-row, per-input-group min/delta scale pages are already in the exact 122,975,884-byte code-page image (1.650544760 BPW, held complete-model NLL 4.498337), before the separate 35,456-byte shared-sign dictionary. The same scale-page delta applies to that cheaper signed-coordinate image. This experiment tests whether *the same scale words* are cheaper as compressed bitplanes or separated low/high byte planes, without changing trits, signs, norms, page geometry or the 16-KiB code pages.

Every one of the 18,192 independent scale pages was compared against both zlib-1-transformed coordinates. An oracle selects the smallest of the old min/delta page, sixteen LSB-first bitplanes compressed with zlib-1, and low-byte/high-byte planes compressed with zlib-1. The actual saved hybrid pays one tag byte per page and a 32-bit offset per page plus end offset, with the same per-matrix random page addressing. All **4,656,128 scale words** were independently reconstructed from the **saved** hybrid image and checked against the original FP16 bits; the old pages were also decoded independently. The unchanged code-page image prefix and norms are retained, so the complete model and its 4.498337 held NLL are identical by image equality, not by a newly estimated loss.

| Complete physical image | Pages selecting min/delta / bitplane / byteplane | Paid scale bytes | Complete bytes | BPW |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Selected code-page image with min/delta scales | 18,192 / 0 / 0 | 6,587,332 | **122,975,884** | **1.650544760** |
| Tagged pagewise oracle image | 18,087 / 91 / 14 | 6,603,514 | **122,992,066** | **1.650761950** |

Only 105 pages benefit from either zlib coordinate. Granting a free page selector and the same existing directory, their combined maximum physical saving is **2,010 bytes / 0.000026978 BPW**. Paying one tag per page instead **adds 16,182 bytes / 0.000217190 BPW** to the complete image. This upper bound applies to these two transforms at zlib level 1 and the pinned 256-row pages, not arbitrary scale coding or learned representations. A bitmask would still need to identify which of three arms a page uses; even a hypothetical one-bit selector costs 2,274 bytes before coding its three states. The extra zlib inflation and indirect addressing have no measured native benefit, and the hybrid is **not selected**. The physical loser image is retained only as evidence, not as a reader to port.

Reproduce on the CPU from Kelana with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 research/ternary/exact-rate/scale_bitplane.py`. No GPU, Bonsai runtime, model selection or resident service changed.

**Next question.** Do not extend static scale bitshuffles at this page size on the strength of exponent structure: the existing delta coordinate captures almost all of this transform family's storage opportunity. Spend new ternary work on genuinely coupled trit/scale whole-model recovery against fresh held text, and price a direct compressed-code consumer before suggesting storage shrinkage is inference speed. For Qwen3.6 MoE, the relevant independent work remains a *paid complete model* learned on broad actual routed producers, or measured ordinary Q8/expert device time; this 0.6B scale result does not transfer its quality or rate to MoE.
