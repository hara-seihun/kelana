# Seven router-score coordinates survive the actual complete routed sum

For **all 5,120** layer/token observations in the two disjoint 64-token Qwen3.6-35B-A3B captures, the eight captured 2,048-component routed down outputs are **affinely independent**. The result is exact for the eight saved FP32 vectors interpreted as dyadic rationals: a nonzero 7×7 determinant modulo 251 witnesses rank seven of their seven differences. The first seven output coordinates alone certify 5,092 cases; later disjoint seven-coordinate minors certify the other 28. Every captured selected score is positive (minimum 0.00893499); the maximum deviation of its eight-score sum from one is 1.49e-7. [The complete 5,120-witness receipt](/path/to/workspace/data/qwen-moe/score-observation-rank/receipt.json) includes each prime, output offset and nonzero residue, all 240 capture-array hashes, source and capture-receipt hashes. Reproduce on CPU with `python3 research/moe/score-observation-rank/rank.py` from the Kelana root.

**Observation contract and theorem.** Fix one selected eight-ID route and its eight captured down outputs `y_0,...,y_7`, and let only the *normalized positive router scores* vary in an open neighborhood of the captured FP32 score vector **renormalized by its measured positive sum** in the seven-dimensional simplex. The renormalization identifies the ideal-real center; it is not a claim that the saved FP32 scores sum to exactly one bitwise. The required real-arithmetic output is the **whole weighted sum**, not eight separate scores or expert channels:

```
F(a_1,...,a_7) = y_0 + sum_{j=1}^7 a_j (y_j - y_0),
a_0 = 1 - sum_{j=1}^7 a_j.
```

Every witnessed determinant makes `DF` rank seven. If a differentiable score-only carrier `E: U -> R^r` and differentiable downstream consumer `R` satisfy `F = R ∘ E` on this open simplex neighborhood, the chain rule requires **r ≥ 7**. Including the unchanged shared-expert output as an additive term leaves this derivative intact. Seven selected-logit differences in the existing [router observer](../router-observer/README.md) are thus dimension-minimal for *these actual complete output observations*, not just for reproducing the eight score scalars. This is not a requirement to materialize intermediate scores: a program may carry seven packed labels directly into a compatible consumer.

**Certificate arithmetic.** Every finite binary32 number times `2^149` is an integer. For a normal float with exponent field `E` and 23-bit fraction `f`, that integer is `(-1)^sign (2^23+f) 2^(E-1)`; for a subnormal it is `(-1)^sign f`. Form the seven difference columns from these integers, reduce modulo 251 and eliminate the selected 7×7 minor. A nonzero determinant modulo a prime is a nonzero integer determinant, hence rank seven over the rationals/reals. No floating singular-value threshold or extrapolation across uncaptured inputs is used. The executable also rejects NaNs/infinities, nonpositive scores and duplicate selected IDs. The first nonzero minor among 32 disjoint seven-output-coordinate attempts is retained per observation. All 5,120 succeed already modulo 251.

**Cost and scope.** This is a semantic lower bound of seven *smooth real score dimensions*, not seven exponentials, seven FP32 registers, seven memory transactions, an ISA-independent operation count or a whole-model speedup. It fixes each output vector while varying weights; it does not restrict a joint producer/score representation on the reachable manifold, discontinuous finite packed labels, approximation, or a consumer that exploits the subsequent layer rather than the complete sum. The saved down outputs reflect the installed GGUF runtime, but the affine sum is interpreted over exact reals; it does not certify the selected FP32 multiply/add order. A Qwen score-only dimension reduction below seven cannot be justified by claiming that the weighted sum happens to collapse selected weights on these captures. The independent forty-layer [seven-exp replay](../../../../bonsai-halo/docs/qwen-moe-router-seven-exp.md) found the entire traced top-k phase only 1.455% of summed device duration, so a native score shortcut is not the present highest-value engine target. The next consequential experiment is ordinary **unprofiled Q8 and expert physical-time attribution** with complete heads; a changed paid representation must instead learn jointly from broader routed producers and pass held complete-model loss and direct-consumer cost.
