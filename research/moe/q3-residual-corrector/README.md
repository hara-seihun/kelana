# A paid producer-conditioned correction cannot rescue frozen Q3 experts

The complete layer-0 routed sum from the frozen native Q3_K gate/up recode differs from decoded installed Q4_K by **.084997 held relative RMS** on 126 actual producer/router tokens (113 disjoint train). Rather than another gain on each expert's existing output direction, this experiment gives the eight-expert sum a new, shared output direction depending on the actual 2,048-dimensional producer input. A rank-r FP16 image stores two `[2048,r]` factors and computes `Q3sum(x) + (x A) B`, with scores and routes entering the Q3sum as before. No fitting uses held tokens. The left factor is ridge-fitted to train error coordinates, the right factor is the top-r SVD basis of train complete-sum errors. Ridge strength is selected by exact leave-one-out squared error on train coordinates (nine fixed positive strengths, from 1e-6 to 100 times the top producer Gram eigenvalue). Both factors are rounded to FP16 before evaluation.

The oracle column gives a **free per-held-token coefficient choice** within the frozen train-error subspace. Its squared-error optimal orthogonal projection cannot be beaten by *any* producer-to-coefficient function using that same basis, even with arbitrary nonlinear computation and hindsight. At rank 112 the .077126 floor remains far from 1% local fidelity, while the actually paid ridge predictor recovers only a small fraction of the Q3 error. This bounds only a fixed train-error-output basis on these inputs, not new Q3 codes, a broader trained basis or a downstream language-loss observer. The trained FP16 image is an explicit paid candidate, not a native mixed reader. Its extra two projections require `2*2048*r` scalar products per layer/token before quantization, scheduling and output addition; their time was not measured. The rate column subtracts forty copies of the FP16 correction image from the previous 89,128,960-byte conditional Q3 gate/up one-read reduction over a 2,626,187,904-byte/token modeled stream. It does not measure actual DRAM or TPS.

It consumes the existing [Q3 per-slot vectors](../q3-scalar-correction/README.md) and [real producer captures](../../../../bonsai-halo/docs/qwen-moe-routes.md). No model payload is committed. No Qwen runtime, selected image, service or GPU state changed.
