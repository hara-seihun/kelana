# Rounded Qwen router weights still depend on omitted logits

The seven-exponential construction is exact over the reals. It is not an exact replacement for the selected runtime's floating-point route. This experiment gives a concrete dependency witness rather than inferring one from a different top-k order.

The observation is the ordered eight IDs and their normalized FP32 weights. The declared host grammar casts the actual layer-0 F32 router dot products on captured producer inputs to FP32, subtracts the maximum, evaluates NumPy FP32 exponentials, sums 256 values in FP32, multiplies each exponential by the reciprocal, sums the eight selected probabilities in FP32 and multiplies them by that reciprocal. This is **not** a bit-level reproduction of the HIP warp reductions or `expf`. Its purpose is to test whether the selected eight logits alone determine this rounded map.

For each of 113 train and 126 held captured inputs, keep all eight selected FP32 logits and their IDs unchanged, and decrease each of the other 248 logits by exactly one FP32 unit of logit value. The selected IDs remain top eight in both inputs. Nevertheless the rounded normalized scores change in 112/113 train and 125/126 held rows, across 533 and 590 score coordinates. Using the same eight captured down vectors, the maximum relative L2 change of the weighted expert sum is 2.241e-7 train and 1.850e-7 held. The first held row supplies all selected input/output FP32 bit patterns and the 0.081954 eighth-to-ninth starting margin in [the receipt](/path/to/workspace/data/qwen-moe/router-observer/precision.json). All model, inventory, capture and script hashes are there too.

This is a direct impossibility witness for *this declared FP32 grammar on unrestricted logit vectors*: any deterministic consumer that receives only the eight selected FP32 logits and IDs must give the same answer on the two witnessed vectors, while the reference grammar gives different FP32 weights. It cannot reproduce that reference bit-for-bit on both vectors. The real cancellation proof fails because rounding the 256-way probability before the second normalization retains a dependence on its first denominator. This does not prove an ISA-independent lower bound of 256 exponentials. A different algorithm may calculate the rounded observation with fewer operations while retaining enough information about the omitted logits. Nor does this witness establish that the modified logit vector is a reachable producer state, that the installed HIP kernel has these exact output bits, or that the 1e-7 local difference affects language quality. No full-model speedup or quality improvement is claimed.

Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=4 /path/to/workspace/data/fish-s2-pro/venv/bin/python research/moe/router-observer/precision.py`. It reads the pinned model and captured layer-0 routes without a GPU. If exact installed logits remain the objective, retain an omitted-logit-dependent denominator or prove that a cheaper implementation reproduces native rounding on the target domain. The independent native experiment with seven exponentials should instead be judged as an explicit approximate map on complete-model held loss, greedy near ties, prompt and decode time. The observed local changes are small enough to justify that experiment, not to declare it compatible in advance.
