# A new right value basis for quantized upstream inputs

The layer-14 narrow V/O image trained on original inputs suffered a large held causal post-O error after layers 0–13 were replaced with the refined binary body and norms. Refitting its left codes and scales on the changed inputs brought held relative squared error to .484360 at 192 coordinates. This experiment relearns the **right** value basis on the same four quantized-producer train windows, then refits the paid output codes against the causal consumer. At the same 183,552 parameter bytes, the selected-rank image scores **.336551** on four held validation windows. The equally trained uniform-rank-24 control scores .336175. The new basis matters much more than the old rank allocation; the .000376 held gap between these arms does not justify choosing unequal ranks.

| 192-coordinate arm | Old right basis, refitted left held | New right basis before causal left fit held | New right basis, two causal sweeps train / held |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original-producer selected ranks | .484360 | .443429 | .008251 / **.336551** |
| Uniform rank 24 | .492553 | .440092 | .009005 / **.336175** |

The held per-window errors are [.304919, .611981, .310459, .114353] for selected and [.305806, .608525, .316387, .109063] for uniform. Each arm improves on all four windows relative to its old-right counterpart. The difficult second window falls from .951162 to .611981 for selected, but the train/held gap remains large. Nothing here establishes fresh-text model loss.

For group `g`, let `X` be the actual quantized-producer train activations, `V_g` the original value projection and `O_pair` the vertically stacked output slices of both query heads. QR of `X V_g^T = Q R` and the thin SVD of `R O_pair^T` give the optimal rank-`r` approximation to **the stacked, unmixed train responses** over the reals. The right coordinates are `(R^-1 U_r)^T V_g`. This conditional SVD does not optimize causal post-O loss. We balance coordinate amplitudes, quantize both factors to the stored signed two-bit grids with FP16 scales, and repair the right factor by weight-domain least squares after quantizing the left. The resulting value responses are BF16-rounded before the original-Q/K causal attention mixture. Two train-only coordinate sweeps fit the two-bit left codes and FP16 scales to the full 1,024-dimensional post-O target. Both images are reopened and the paid left decoder is checked against its fitted coefficients.

The basis construction is shared by the two heads of each GQA group, and their distinct attention weights remain separate. No original 128-wide value is reconstructed online. Both arms still store **183,552 bytes, .466797 BPW over V/O**, use 589,824 signed-grid factor terms per token and retain 384 logical BF16 narrow-value bytes per token. This is a CPU quality replay with original layer-14 Q/K and teacher V/O evaluated on the *same* quantized-producer input, not full-model NLL or a native kernel timing. Training used four 256-token train windows; validation used four distinct 256-token windows. The older left-refit control received the same two sweeps and byte/term budget. The only added work is offline basis fitting and quantization.

`right_transfer.py selected192` and `right_transfer.py uniform192` regenerate the new images and receipts. Use `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1` with `/path/to/workspace/data/fish-s2-pro/venv/bin/python`. The data owner has `layer14-quantized-upstream-right-{selected192,uniform192}.{npz,json}`. Each receipt gives all window errors, paid bytes, source/input/model/quantizer/image hashes and the observation contract. The inputs and old-right controls are the immutable files from [producer transfer](PRODUCER-TRANSFER.md).
